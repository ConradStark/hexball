## Pillars (13 Nov — every mechanic is checked against these; when they clash, the lower number wins)
1. **Moments of magic, both ways.** A well-planned attack cuts through to a clear chance; a correct read in defence wins the ball. Not a game where you score all the time: **a close contest either side can win, and winning the ball turns you straight into the attack**, like real football.
   - *Attack:* the give-and-go puts you clear on goal at a high %. A pass out wide, the defender lunges and misses, the cross is in, a free header in the box… and it's good!
   - *Defence:* "I think he's going there": block the space, track the runner, step into the lane, get rewarded for the prediction. "I know the header's coming": mark the man, here it comes, go for the clearance… got it!
2. **Grounded in real football.** Movement, passing and shooting behave like the real thing; tweaked for fun only where it rewards good football (e.g. a bonus for linking play).
3. **Reward movement, don't restrict it.** Make moving and attacking pay off instead of limiting what you can do. No stagnant play.

### Rules of thumb (the old guiding principles, sorted under the pillars)
- **Under 1:** You can see your options and how likely they are before you commit (move range, pass range ground/lob, the hexes each defender threatens, the chance as a word/colour) — you can't be rewarded for a prediction you couldn't see. No stacked hidden modifiers; skills change something you can see. Once beaten, a defender stays beaten. A correct read deserves a moment; a mini-game only when the result is really in doubt (near-certain ones just happen). Make combination play feel like magic. You always know what happened and why.
- **Under 2:** Everything moves square by square, defenders included (a pass beats a man who is too far away). One plan per turn: runs start together, touches happen in order. Respect real football where it matters (kick-off, keeper restarts, onside). Dice are fine — football is chance-based. **Decided 13 Nov: odds are shown as words/colours (Safe / Risky / Long shot), not %; the board (threats, reach) is the forecast. Behind it, contests roll 2RN-style (Fire Emblem's "true hit": average of two rolls, so a 70% wins ~80%, a 25% ~15%) — good positioning is reliable, long shots rarer.** No pity bags for now (only if play-tests show bad streaks, e.g. in duels). Exact % only in the ⓘ detail mode, if at all.
- **Under 3:** Encourage passing over carrying. Choices are trade-offs you can feel (ground pass = one more kick; lob = distance but a control touch), not flat bonuses. UI minimal: actions go straight into the plan, one Lock in, pop-ups only for choices and never blocking the view, nothing left on the board when a turn ends.
- **How we work:** validate by playing — small rule changes, then play-test; big rule changes as a start-screen option first; one change per release, rhythm numbers before/after.

**Core loops (verbs):** attack **Plan → Move → Pass → Shoot**; defence **Predict → React → Tackle / Steal → Counter**. Gaps now: seeing range and pass risk (attack); steal (jumping a lane) isn't a readable choice; counter (winning it and going) barely exists as something you see or feel. Idea from Into the Breach: the attack telegraphs (e.g. a held pass shows its arrow once the passer is set) so the defender's response is a readable decision. Pillar checks on what's there: clearances are a 25% roll after winning the ball anywhere — pillar 1 wants the clearance as the defender's choice in his box; ~1 turn in 5 is quiet (pillar 3).

**Order of work (one release each, rhythm numbers before/after):** (v2.26 done; v2.27 = interceptions only on the line, user's call) 1) visibility (no rule change, replay identical) → 2) odds as words + 2RN-style contests + mini-game threshold (only the really-in-doubt contests get a duel) → 3) defender option B + the lean ladder (Anticipate: full hex / edge / corner) → 4) loops one verb at a time: steal, then counter.

**Tuning targets (13 Nov, draft — what "feels right" means, checked with `npm run rhythm`):** a sensible pass arrives 85–90%, a risky one 50–65% · a read the ball reaches wins 50–60% (now ~70%) · about half of spells reach a shot (now 42–47%) · quiet turns under 10% (now 17–22%) · mini-games under half of today's (now ~33/match).

**Decided 13 Nov — defender movement, option B (to build as a start-screen option):** every defender can move **up to 2 hexes per React window** (matches runners' 1 hex a frame over the 2-frame clock; a pass still beats him), and **at most 3 defenders move per window** (matches the attack's 3 moving players). Replaces the step pool (5, then 2 + unused, max 3), so the danger zone is the same 2-hex ring every time. Rejected: A (everyone moves 2: too many taps, too strong), C (keep the pool: zone changes shape every window).

## Current rules (v2.34)

### Setup
- **Start screen (v2.0, from the user's sketch):** hex wordmark (the H's stem and the final l are the hexagon's sides; the slanted edges stop short so the letters stand alone; small ball in the top). **vs CPU / vs Friend** toggle (Friend = hotseat), **Team size** dropdown (**7v7 default**, 6v6, 5v5), **Pitch** dropdown (v2.23: **9 × 13 · goal 3** default, or **10 × 15 · goal 4**: box 6 wide × 3 deep, 2-hex wings, no middle column in goal so the keeper starts on one of the middle two), **You start** dropdown (Random default / Attacking = you kick off / Defending = their kick-off), **Kick off**. **More** (collapsed): Load state (paste) and Presets (Attack in progress; Right-wing cross · defend it; · both sides — all 7v7 positions; more presets for testing to come).
- 5v5 line-up: H1, H5 (centre back 4,10), H6 2,8, H10 6,8, H9 · A1, A4 (4,2), A7 2,4, A11 6,4, A9. Config: `DROP` / `FORMS` per size.
- Stats PAS, SHO, DRI, DEF, REF (keeper). Rarity rings: common, rare, epic, legendary.
- **Game screen (v2.1, from the user's sketch):** header = Hex FC badge (tap → main menu), score, turn. Under the board: Undo left, Play right (defence: Watch the rest / Go), a one-line hint. Below: the plan's steps, each with × (removes that step and every step after it). **More** (collapsed): Retry my last attack (back to the start of your last attacking turn, new dice; hotseat: last turn), Win all duels (practice: your side wins every duel/contest; keeper saves, accuracy and scatter still roll), Main menu, **Match feed** (readable turn cards: plan chips, Prepare/Move/Mark, duels with % and who won, shots with on-target and save %, result), Technical log (copy/paste for Claude), How a turn works.

### A turn
1. **Plan** (attacker, hidden) → 2. **Prepare** (defender sees the players, not the plan) → 3. **Playback** in frames with **React windows** for the defence → turn ends (keep ball / turnover / restart / goal).
- Speeds per frame: players 1 hex, ground pass 3 hexes, lob lands after 3 frames, shot instant once runs finish. Defenders step in the same frame as attackers.

### Attack
- **One pass or shot a turn** (v2.11): **v2.30: one Pass, ground or air by distance — 1–4 hexes along the ground, 5–8 in the air (`GROUND_MAX`, `passKind`)**; max 8, through ball, Give & Go, Cross card, or shot. **v2.24: a ground pass gives the receiver one more pass or shot**, played first time (no control frame: the ball goes on the next frame); a lob needs the control touch and ends the kicking. The second kick can't be a card (Give & Go / Cross are first kicks only); no third. **Dribbles** (≤2 hexes) are free of that limit but count as one of the 3 moving players: dribble then pass/shoot, or pass and the receiver dribbles on. A shot ends the ball's turn.
- **Up to 3 moving players** (runs and dribbles), runs 3 hexes each, all starting together; runs can be added after the pass. One player may **move twice** (second run ≤2), which makes him **tired** (−1 hex) on his team's next attacking turn.
- A defender on your hex takes 1 off your run/dribble. Mid-play, runners go through players who step in their way; only a teammate still standing on the last hex stops a run. You can target a teammate's hex if he's running away that turn.
- **Pass vs through ball:** a pass to a runner is **held** until it will arrive with him (passer can be pressed/tackled meanwhile, and the defence gets a window). A **through ball** goes now, to the run's end, and sits loose until he arrives — first one there wins it.
- **Control touch:** after a lob the receiver needs 1 frame before his next touch ("Control"); a controlled lob finishes as a **volley**, never a header. v2.24: after a ground pass he plays it on first time (pass or shot).
- **Cards** (v2.32: **Give & Go is the only card**; it rests for the team's next attacking turn. The Cross is a normal option, see Planning):
  - **Give & Go:** the turn's one pass: wall pass, return run up to 4, return pass (he can't shoot after it that turn).
  - **Cross:** from the outer two columns to a teammate ≤3 from goal; lob → header (−22% on target, keeper −8%), low → first-time shot (+18%, keeper −25%).
- Scattered pass: the intended receiver picks it up if he has movement to spare; if he can't, a defender standing on that hex takes it; otherwise it's loose (v2.27: the v2.6 pounce from next door is gone). Still a scatter turnover (passer not beaten).
- **Planning (v2.28, v2.34):** tap the man on the ball → his **dribble hexes** show (no Move / Pass toggle). Tap one = dribble there; **then nothing stays selected** (v2.34: the next tap picks a player to move). Tap him to pass from there; **tap his hex again while he's selected = the dribble becomes a pass into that space** (the space window), tap it again = back to the dribble. Tap the ball carrier then a teammate = a pass. **Pass window (v2.30, v2.32): two choices at most — Through, and Pass (or Cross when the passer is wide and a runner is within 3 of goal).** Cross → **Shoot** (first time: goal picker) or **Wait** (a normal pass he controls). A normal pass to a runner = plan his run first, then tap the run's end → Pass (held so it arrives with him). Pass first then move him = the ball to his feet, his move a dribble after receiving. Empty hexes beyond his dribble = the space window as before; a teammate = the pass pop-up. (v2.26 had a Move / Pass toggle and range outlines + colour chips; outlines/chips removed in v2.27, toggle in v2.28.) A picked pass: the arrow takes its chance colour (**blue ≥75% safe, amber 50–75% risky, red <50% long shot**) (planned passes too), lane hexes a defender can contest turn red, he gets a red ring (no % bubbles). Exact % stays in the ground/air pop-up. **Danger toggle** (⚠ button top right of the board while planning, remembered on the device): shades every hex a defender can reach before the next window (`defReach`, `ORDER_MAX` 2), darker where 2+ can, with a red outline.
- Ground passes are drawn on the straight line passer → receiver at one speed; a lane defender leans in for the contest (v2.5, drawing only).

### Defence
- **Two modes (v2.25, start screen → Defence):** **Respond is the standard since v2.34** (Prepare = "Prepare (old)"; saved states without `def` still load as Prepare). Respond: the attack moves first — no Prepare, the first React window comes after frame 1, then every 2 frames (after 1, 3, 5…), so a first-time shot at frame 1 still goes off. In Respond a selected outfield defender also gets **Anticipate** (one at a time, free): he reads the play — a pass, through ball, lob landing, dribble or shot lane that comes through **his own hex** (v2.27; was his hex + the six round it) gets **+20%** on his contest ("Read it!"); it stays set while his team defends (across turns, like a mark) until it's used; shown as a gold area + dashed ring to both sides, and in the attacker's pass/shot odds. Next to the man on the ball (ball at his feet): **Tackle** (2 steps, steps in, the tackle is a timing game for a human: the green zone's width = his win chance, tap in it = win, outside = beaten), **Press** (1 step: his next pass +12% scatter, shot −10% on target), **Block** (1 step: steps into his line to goal, +15% on that block). Undo covers them.
- **Prepare** (after the reveal, before frame 1): move up to **2 players 1 hex** each, free, keeper included. Not on a counter. Stepping onto a loose ball wins it at once (v2.14).
- **React windows** (v2.8): on a clock, every 2 frames of play. Steps per window: first **5** (2 on a counter), later **2 + unused last time, max 3**. Up to 3 players sent per window, each ≤2 hexes. Orders are walked while play goes on. **Undo** (v2.14) takes back the last move, order or mark in the window until Go.
- **Clearances** (v2.8): a defender who wins a contest clears it 60% in his own box, 25% elsewhere: 3–4 hexes away from his goal, the play stops (loose = race next turn).
- **Tackle:** a defender on the carrier's hex, costs 2 steps. **Mark:** 1 attacker within 3 hexes; the marker follows goal side for free each frame; moving him breaks the mark.
- **Keeper** moves inside his box (Prepare/React); returns to his line when his team attacks. **With his hands** (v2.16–v2.18), in his box only: claims a pass to or through his hex 75% (65% in the air), smothers a dribbler/passer on his hex 55%, rushes out at a dribbler who comes next to him 30% (beat him → he's out of the play). He holds what he claims (no clearance).
- A defender who moved this turn gets **+12%** in his contests (commit bonus).
- **Beaten:** the attacker who lost the ball sits out the next turn (no contests, no Prepare pick; can still recover in React). Not after a scattered pass.

### Winning the ball
- **v2.27: only a defender ON the ball's path, or on the passer's / receiver's hex, when the ball gets there can win it** (stepping into the path in time still counts). Next to the lane or receiver does nothing: no pressure scatter, no pounce on a scattered ball, no Anticipate jump from next door. Before v2.27: only a defender blocking the path or contesting could win it. Ground pass: on a lane hex 40%, on the receiver 55%, on the passer 20%. Lob: on the receiver 55%, passer 12%. Dribble: on the carrier 45% ± (DEF − DRI)/200. Shot: on the shooter 35%, in the lane 30%. (v2.27: no pressure scatter from next to the lane/receiver.)
- Collision ring first, then the duel (timing tap +5/+3/+1%). Contests under 15% resolve quietly with a word (Nicked it! / Near miss / Gets by).
- Ball winner is drawn ahead on a shared hex until he moves; otherwise the defender is drawn goal side. No push-back.

### Shots (v2.15–v2.18; numbers per set-up on the Shot Situations page; v2.31: no "close" blocks — only a defender on the shooter's hex or on the line to the aimed spot blocks)
- **On target** = goal-mouth angle × 1.2% per degree (~98% six-yard box, 75% spot, 40% from 30 m) + SHO; top row −10%, centre +5%, near post +8%, far post −25% from wide (scaled by how wide); −8% per defender next to the shooter, level or goal side only (v2.22; max 3).
- **Blocks** (one combined duel): on the shooter 35%, on the line to the aimed side 30%, next to him (level or goal side) 22% on every spot, 2 away goal side 12% on every spot. Late defenders lunge ("Too late!") but can't block; the keeper doesn't lunge (his dive is the save).
- **Keeper save:** in line 72%, one column off 46%, two off 5% at point blank rising to 20% from the edge of the box; +5% a row from outside the box; top corners −8% (−5% more per row off his line), low shots +6% per row off his line; REF and distance when he's near. **Closing the angle:** next to the shooter (level or goal side) he covers the corners as if half as far, 2 in front 90%. **Behind the shooter = 0%.**
- **Crit strikes:** 3% + (SHO−70)/400, +3% top corners (5–12%); always on target, keeper's chance halved.
- Curl: from wide the ball bends towards the middle first, then out to the corner (v2.14).
- **Shot picker (v2.4):** the goal mouth filled with colour = chance of scoring (aim × past blockers × beating the keeper, his likely position weighted with the hexes he can reach in one step), six spots, tap to shoot. ⓘ = detail mode: High/Med/Low per spot; tap a spot → panel (aim, blockers, keeper save range, crit on top corners, near/far post, goal chance; cross: lands) + Shoot here. Cut-offs: aim 50/75%, keeper 20/45%, goal 30/55%. Crosses use the same picker.
- Assists: gone (v2.19); with one pass or shot a turn only the Cross card sets up a finish (`CROSS_AS`: lob = header −22% on target, keeper −8%; low = first-time shot +18%, keeper −25%).
- Saves: keeper restarts from where he caught it (players make room); attackers in his box move to the top of the box; then **onside** (v2.2, the only offside rule; v2.10: also on every turnover, the man on the ball stays): his team's players beyond the other side's deepest outfield player come back level with him. Wide: goal kick (same).
- Look: top corners curl outside-in, keeper dives, slow motion (2.5×) with a vignette, word pops (Shot!/Header!/Volley!, Saved!, Fingertips…).

### CPU
- **Attack (v1.33):** 2–3 runs with jobs (target, support, width), then every 1–2-touch combo scored by goal chance and turnover risk, picked with some randomness (more at kick-off).
- **Defence (v1.34):** scores reachable hexes for pressing, passing lanes, shot lane, loose balls; marks the biggest threat; keeper lines up with the ball; tackles at ≥45%.

## Recent decisions (newest first)
- **v2.67** (27 Nov) **Tests brought up to date + two fixes they found.** `test/` had stopped at v2.47 (v2.29–v2.34 skipped full runs and nothing caught up): 18 failures, all tests describing old rules (Respond default, close blocks gone, Cross not a card and winger-only, dribble/space taps, one Pass button, Wall, board defending, read ring on the token, tackle queued on Go, Reshape step). Rewritten to the current rules; replay check now expects Respond's odd-frame windows; baseline re-recorded. ## Idea board
- **Anticipate between hexes** (user, 13 Nov): instead of the 7-hex read (his hex + the 6 round it, +20%), put the defender **on a corner between 3 hexes**: he covers those 3 at a lower bonus than a read on one exact hex. Also "prepare for a pass in a direction". Note: the 3 hexes at a corner are his own hex + 2 neighbours, so a corner = a lean in one of 6 directions — one mechanic covers both. Narrower guess, bigger reward (pillar 1); should bring the ~70% read success down. User, 13 Nov: **three read sizes — full hex (biggest bonus), edge between two hexes (half), corner between three (smallest)**; picked by a drag or an option after tapping Anticipate ("pick a direction if you want"). Open: bonus sizes, does the lean count as a move.
- **Odds display, simpler** (user, 13 Nov): forecast % (mock V4) changes as soon as the defence moves; prefer fewer stats and "let the game talk". → Decided: words/colours + 2RN-style rolls (see Rules of thumb, Under 2). The 2-hex zone shows where he can be by the next window (runs, held passes, where a receiver ends up); stealing a ground pass comes from being there already (positioning / Anticipate).
